What to do next

Buy three cheap samples and measure them: the 6 V 3 W 145 × 145 mm ($5.99), the 5.5 V 1.3 W 147 × 90 mm ($4.02) and the TZT 6 V 1.92 W 140 × 104 mm ($3.19). That costs about $13 plus shipping. Sellers' wattage figures are optimistic: one buyer measured the "1.92 W" TZT panel at about 0.85 W.

Choosing between a 5 V and a 6 V panel waits on one charger question (section 4). A 6 V panel's open-circuit voltage sits right at the BQ25798's possible 7 V start-up limit.

4 · Energy and voltage

How much the node can run on

Average power the panel can sustain = rated W × 4 peak-sun hours × 75 % harvest ÷ 24 h, using the same estimates as docs/power-budget.md. With every radio listening, the node draws about 1.8 W (estimate), shown as the dashed line.

Even the 5 W Voltaic panel sustains about a third of the always-listening load. Any handheld panel means the node sleeps most of the time (open question Q-13).

Open-circuit voltage against the charger's start-up limit

The BQ25798 cuts off an input above its VAC_OVP threshold. The datasheet is self-contradictory on the power-on default: 7 V in the field table, 26 V by the register reset value (p.72). If it is 7 V (6.7–7.3 V, p.10), a dead node can't charge from a panel above it. Only panels with a published or measured Voc are shown. Voc also rises in the cold.

The SS34 reverse-protection diode drops about 0.4–0.55 V before the charger input, which helps slightly. The P105 bar shows its ±10 % tolerance from the Voltaic drawing.

6 · On the node

Any DC source plugs into a 24 V barrel jack

The panel, or any other DC source from 3.6 to 24 V, connects from outside the enclosure through a DC-005G jack on the board edge: a 5.5 × 2.1 mm plug, centre pin positive. LCSC C692446, $0.16, rated 24 V 2.5 A; firmware caps the input at 2.4 A. A P-FET blocks a reversed plug, an SMAJ24A clamps surges, and a 2 Ω + 2.2 µF snubber handles hot plugging. Every panel needs a 5.5 × 2.1 mm plug on its cable; the AliExpress "Cable" sizes come with bare wires.

7 · Decisions waiting

What this depends on

  1. T17a: settle the BQ25798 VAC_OVP power-on default with TI or on a board. 26 V allows any of these panels; 7 V points to a 5 V panel.
  2. Q-13: accept a node that sleeps most of the time, or choose a bigger panel.
  3. Samples: measure Voc, Isc and the power curve of the three cheap panels in sun before choosing.
  4. T20: rework the solar input for a 6 V panel (no snubber needed; weigh the SS34 drop), and add ESD/TVS protection at the external jack.
