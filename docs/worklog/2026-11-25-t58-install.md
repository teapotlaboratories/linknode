## T64 section 4 (power), started 2026-11-25
- Scope from DRC (main 14d77dd): 354 open links on 112 nets, essentially the whole power section. Sub-sections 4a–4f in todo T64.
- Layout rules read:
  - TLV62569 §10.1 (p.13): caps and L as close as possible; short, wide power traces; cap grounds to power GND; FB away from SW.
  - TPS61089 §11.1 (p.22): minimise SW length/area; ground plane under the converter; CIN near VIN/GND; the loop SW FET → rectifier → COUT → GND kept short.
  - BQ25798 Rev C §8.4.1 (p.139), priority list: 0.1 µF on PMID/SYS at the IC; SW1/SW2 to L through multiple vias, minimum area; REGN/VBUS cap grounds ≥2 vias; BATP/TS away from SW.
