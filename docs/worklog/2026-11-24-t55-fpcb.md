## microSD route vs the RV1106 Hardware Design Guide (owner asked, 2026-11-24)
Source: `resources/resource/Rockchip-RV1103G-RV1106G-Hardware-Design-Guide-en-V1-1-20221221.pdf` (HDG): Table 2-7 p.24, §3.2 items (3) (5) (6) (9) (11) (16) (21)–(25) p.39–46, §3.3.1 + Table 3-1 p.46–47. Main board (SD bus identical on s7/b2).
- Meets:
  - 50 Ω (0.17 mm, T53).
  - Length: pin to contact via `pathlen.py`. CLK is 22.84 + 2.69 mm plus R511 ≈ 26.5. Against it: CMD 27.48 (+1.0), D0 24.90 (−1.6), D1 25.07 (−1.4), D2 28.16 (+1.7), D3 27.42 (+0.9). All within 120 mil = 3.05 mm, and far below 4 in.
  - 2W spacing (except the 3 approved necks).
  - ESD: TPD4E05U06 0.5 pF (`hardware/datasheets/TI_TPD4E05U06.pdf` §6.3.4 p.12), next to J401. The flow-through layout gives no stubs.
  - 22 Ω on CLK (R511) present.
  - 45° bends.
- Deviations:
  1. **Reference plane:** the bottom bus runs over the In2 **VDD_0V9 island** (131.3–155.3 × 118.2–143.2), not over GND, and not over the 5 V plane as project rules says. Measured per net: CMD 23.0 mm over 0V9, 0.7 over 5V, 1.0 over the zone gap; D0–D3 and CLK are similar. Every line crosses the 0V9 → 5V split at y 143.2 just before its via. HDG (3), (6), (25) and §3.3.1 all say GND reference, with no crossing between planes.
  2. **R511 placement:** at the card end (144.62, 142.4 B), 2.7 mm from J401 and 22.8 mm from U100. HDG (9): place series resistors near the transmitter.
  3. **No GND shielding:** none on CLK alone, none on the DATA/CMD group (§3.3.1, (21)/(22)).
  4. **Escape vias with no nearby GND via:** the CMD, D3, D2 and CLK escape vias at the RV1106 have their nearest GND via 2.11, 1.95, 1.55 and 1.58 mm away (HDG (16); project rules wants one beside each).
  5. **Same-layer GND pour clearance:** 0.2 mm, against the ≥ 3W = 0.51 mm recommended by (5).
  6. **Y502 (32.768 kHz) crystal:** D1 on the bottom passes 0.24 mm from its outline (HDG (11)). In1 GND lies between them.
- Feasibility of an In2 GND corridor under the bus: no VDD_0V9 or VCC5V0_SYS via or PTH pad lies within 1 mm of any bottom bus segment. It is a zone-only change: the 0V9 island loses its SW margin, and the 5 V plane loses a strip at y 143–146.
- Bus speed context: VCCIO4 is fixed at 3.3 V, so SD 2.0 mode (HDG p.23), 50 MHz max. 28 mm is electrically short, so skew and R511 placement matter little. The plane split matters most (return path, EMI).
